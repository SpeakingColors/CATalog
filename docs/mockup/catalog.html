Splice

No login here, on purpose. Splice has no public API for other apps, so CATalog cannot sign in to your account or browse its catalogue. You search and download in the Splice app or on splice.com; CATalog picks the files up from the folder Splice saves into.

  1. Open Splice and sign in there.
  2. Choose the folder Splice saves its downloads into (see the download location in the Splice app's settings).
  3. Switch on Watch. New files appear in the Library under that folder, analysed and sorted.
